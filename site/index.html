Sponos

A nicer way to play music on Sonos, with Spotify built in.

Download for Mac

Latest version · macOS 12 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · Free

  1. InstallOpen the download and drag Sponos to Applications.
  2. Allow the local networkClick Allow when macOS asks. That's how Sponos finds your speakers.
  3. Connect SpotifyOptional: see your playlists and liked songs. Search works without it.
